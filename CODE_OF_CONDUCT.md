# ZenLab Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, educators, and leaders pledge to make participation in **ZenLab** a harassment-free, welcoming, and safe experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Because ZenLab is an educational platform directly utilized by elementary school children, teachers, and parents, we place the highest priority on maintaining a safe, encouraging, supportive, and kid-appropriate environment across all issues, pull requests, discussions, and community spaces.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward students, educators, and other contributors.
* Encouraging curiosity and constructive experimentation in computer science and AI.
* Being respectful of differing pedagogical viewpoints, classroom realities, and student needs.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
* Using welcoming and inclusive language.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information, such as student names, photos, physical or email addresses, without their explicit permission (violating our strict Zero-PII mandate).
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in an educational or professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, and discussions), and also applies when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting & Contact

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `team@learnai.internal`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
